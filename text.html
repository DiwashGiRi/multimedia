Nature

Nature is the living and physical world around us, including trees, rivers, mountains, and animals that exist without human creation.Mother nature has always nourished us, taken care of us, and provided us with all the necessities of life, as noted by Essay on Nature in English | Paragraph on Nature. It gives us clean air to breathe, fresh water to drink, and food to eat for our survival. The beauty of nature brings peace to our minds and keeps the Earth in balance. However, human activities like pollution and cutting down trees are harming this vital environment. We must protect nature by planting more trees and keeping our surroundings clean.

Nature is a beautiful gift from God. It includes trees, flowers, rivers, mountains, forests, animals, birds, and the sky. Nature makes our world beautiful and gives us fresh air, water, food, and many other things we need to live. Spending time in nature makes us feel happy, peaceful, and relaxed. We should love and protect nature by planting trees, keeping our surroundings clean, and avoiding pollution. If we take care of nature, it will continue to take care of us.
